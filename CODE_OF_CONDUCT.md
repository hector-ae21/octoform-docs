# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, acceptance of
responsibility, and focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable
behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, publishing
private information, and conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the contact methods in the
[Octoform security policy](https://github.com/hector-ae21/octoform/security/policy).
Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communication
that do not align with this code, and will respect the reporter's privacy.
